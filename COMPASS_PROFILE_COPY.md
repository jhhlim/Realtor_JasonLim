### Bio

Jason Lim is a Compass REALTOR® serving San Jose and Silicon Valley. Whether working with first-time buyers taking that exciting leap, move-up homeowners seeking their next chapter, or investors evaluating opportunity, Jason supports clients with a disciplined, data-first approach.

Before real estate, Jason spent 10+ years as a software engineer and AI developer building enterprise tools, automation, and analytics. That background shapes how he works today: transparent analysis, clear communication, and modern tools that help clients understand comps, timing, and risk — without the outdated playbooks.

His process is never one-size-fits-all. Jason combines market analytics with personalized strategy so clients can move with confidence in competitive Bay Area markets.

## THE EXPERIENCE

### Technology Advantage & Strategic Clarity

Jason brings an engineering mindset to every transaction. He uses market data, AI-assisted research, and custom analysis to surface what actually matters — recent comps, neighborhood tradeoffs, offer strategy, and rate implications — so clients can decide with clarity instead of guesswork.

### Personal, Straightforward Approach

By staying responsive and selective about client load, Jason offers a hands-on experience rooted in honest communication. He takes time to understand each client’s goals, timeline, and risk tolerance — and stays available when offers move fast.

With Jason, it’s more than a transaction — it’s a partnership built on trust, precision, and follow-through. Clients benefit from his tech-forward preparation and the powerful platform of COMPASS, one of the most innovative brokerages in the nation. From first conversation to closing, he aims to make the home journey clear, strategic, and well-supported.
